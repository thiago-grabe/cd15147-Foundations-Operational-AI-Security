1. Welcome to Northstar

Our Mission

At Northstar Technologies, our mission is to empower businesses with intelligent data solutions that drive growth and innovation. We believe in making complex data accessible and actionable for organizations of all sizes.

Our Values

  • Customer Obsession: We put our customers at the center of everything we do
  • Innovation: We continuously push boundaries and embrace new ideas
  • Integrity: We act with honesty and transparency in all interactions
  • Collaboration: We achieve more together than we could alone
  • Excellence: We strive for the highest quality in our work

Company Overview

Founded in 2019, Northstar Technologies is a mid-size SaaS company headquartered in Seattle, Washington, with additional offices in Austin, Texas and New York City. We serve over 500 customers across industries including healthcare, finance, retail, and manufacturing.

Our Locations:
  • Seattle (HQ): 1234 Tech Avenue, Suite 500, Seattle, WA 98101
  • Austin: 567 Innovation Drive, Austin, TX 78701

Equal Employment Opportunity

Northstar Technologies is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, veteran status, sexual orientation, gender identity, or any other protected characteristic.

At-Will Employment

Employment at Northstar Technologies is "at-will," meaning either the employee or the company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause, and with or without notice, subject to applicable law.

Background Checks

All offers of employment are contingent upon successful completion of a background check, which may include verification of employment history, education, criminal records, and credit history (where applicable and permitted by law).

3. Workplace Guidelines

Work Schedule

Standard Hours: Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM local time

Core Hours: 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM (required presence for meetings/collaboration)

Flexible Hours: Employees may adjust start/end times with manager approval

Hybrid Work Policy

Northstar offers a hybrid work arrangement for eligible positions:

  • Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week
  • Minimum 2 days per week in-office (typically Tuesday-Thursday)
  • Remote work days should be coordinated with team/manager
  • Fully remote positions available for certain roles (as specified in job description)

Remote Work Requirements

  • Reliable high-speed internet connection
  • Dedicated, quiet workspace
  • Availability during core hours via Slack/email
  • Attendance at scheduled video meetings with camera on
  • Compliance with information security policies

Dress Code

Northstar maintains a business casual dress code. When meeting with customers or external partners, business professional attire may be required. Team-specific guidelines may apply.

4. Compensation & Benefits

Pay Schedule

Employees are paid semi-monthly on the 15th and last day of each month. If a pay date falls on a weekend or holiday, payment will be processed on the preceding business day.

Health Benefits

Medical Insurance

Choice of PPO or HDHP plans through Aetna. Company covers 90% of employee premium and 70% of dependent premiums.

Dental & Vision

Dental coverage through Delta Dental. Vision coverage through VSP. Company covers 100% of employee premium.

Life & Disability Insurance

Basic life insurance equal to 1x annual salary (up to $250,000). Short-term and long-term disability coverage provided at no cost.

Retirement Benefits

401(k) Plan

Northstar offers a 401(k) plan with company match. We match 100% of contributions up to 4% of salary. Immediate vesting on all contributions.

Equity Compensation

Eligible employees may receive stock option grants as part of their total compensation package. Grants are subject to a 4-year vesting schedule with a 1-year cliff. Details are provided in individual grant agreements.

5. Time Off & Leave

Paid Time Off (PTO)

Northstar offers a flexible PTO policy. Full-time employees receive:

  • Years 0-2: 15 days PTO per year
  • Years 3-5: 20 days PTO per year
  • Years 6+: 25 days PTO per year

PTO accrues monthly and may be carried over up to 5 days into the following year. Unused PTO beyond the carryover limit will be forfeited.

Holidays

Northstar observes the following paid holidays:

  • New Year's Day
  • Martin Luther King Jr. Day
  • Presidents' Day
  • Memorial Day
  • Independence Day
  • Labor Day
  • Thanksgiving Day
  • Day after Thanksgiving
  • Christmas Eve
  • Christmas Day
  • 2 Floating Holidays (employee choice)

Sick Leave

Employees accrue 1 hour of sick leave for every 30 hours worked, up to a maximum of 80 hours per year. Sick leave may be used for personal illness, medical appointments, or caring for a sick family member.

Parental Leave

Birth/Adoption Leave: 16 weeks paid leave for primary caregivers, 8 weeks paid leave for secondary caregivers. Leave must be taken within 12 months of birth/adoption.

Bereavement Leave

  • Immediate family (spouse, child, parent, sibling): 5 days paid
  • Extended family (grandparent, in-law, etc.): 3 days paid

Jury Duty

Employees will be paid their regular salary for up to 10 days of jury duty. Proof of jury service may be required.

6. Code of Conduct

Professional Conduct

All employees are expected to:

  • Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect
  • Communicate professionally in all business interactions
  • Maintain confidentiality of sensitive information
  • Avoid conflicts of interest
  • Report concerns or violations promptly

Anti-Harassment Policy

Zero Tolerance: Northstar maintains a zero-tolerance policy for harassment, discrimination, or retaliation of any kind. This includes but is not limited to harassment based on race, gender, religion, sexual orientation, disability, or any other protected characteristic.

Reporting: Incidents should be reported to your manager, HR, or via our anonymous ethics hotline: 1-800-555-ETHICS

Social Media Policy

  • Do not share confidential company information on social media
  • Clearly identify personal opinions as your own, not company views
  • Do not disparage the company, colleagues, customers, or competitors
  • Report any concerns about company-related social media activity to Marketing

Substance Policy

The use, possession, or distribution of illegal drugs on company premises or during work hours is strictly prohibited. Alcohol may be consumed responsibly at company-sponsored events. Employees should not report to work impaired.

7. Information Security

Data Protection

All employees are responsible for protecting company and customer data. This includes:

  • Using strong, unique passwords for all accounts
  • Enabling two-factor authentication where available
  • Not sharing credentials with others
  • Locking computers when away from desk
  • Reporting suspected security incidents immediately

Acceptable Use of Technology

Company-provided equipment and systems should be used primarily for business purposes. Limited personal use is acceptable provided it:

  • Does not interfere with work responsibilities
  • Does not violate company policies or law
  • Does not consume excessive bandwidth or storage
  • Does not expose the company to security risks

Confidential Information

Employees may have access to confidential information including customer data, financial records, product plans, and trade secrets. This information must:

  • Only be accessed on a need-to-know basis
  • Never be shared outside the company without authorization
  • Be properly secured at all times
  • Be returned to the company upon termination

Security Training

All employees must complete annual security awareness training. Additional role-specific training may be required. Training records are maintained by IT Security.
