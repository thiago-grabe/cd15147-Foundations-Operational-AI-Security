COMPANY PERFORMANCE UPDATE

================================================================================
Q3 STRATEGIC PRIORITIES
================================================================================

1. PLATFORM SCALABILITY
   Owner: Sarah Chen, VP of Engineering

   We're investing heavily in platform infrastructure to support our growing
   customer base. Key initiatives include:
   - Migration to Kubernetes (EKS) for improved scalability
   - Database read replica architecture for reporting workloads
   - Enhanced caching layer to reduce API latency

2. ENTERPRISE FEATURE SET
   Owner: Jennifer Williams, VP of Product

   To accelerate enterprise sales, we're prioritizing:
   - Advanced role-based access control (RBAC)
   - Audit logging and compliance reporting
   - Custom SSO integrations
   - Dedicated support tiers

3. MARKET EXPANSION
   Owner: Robert Martinez, VP of Sales

   We're expanding our go-to-market efforts:
   - New partner program launching in August
   - Healthcare vertical focus (HIPAA compliance in progress)
   - EMEA expansion planning for Q4
